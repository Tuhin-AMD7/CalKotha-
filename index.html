How Stamps Work

GPS-Powered Collection

📍

Visit the Site

Travel to a heritage site in Kolkata. Make sure location services are enabled.

📡

GPS Detects You

When you're within 200m of a heritage site, CalKotha automatically detects your presence.

📮

Stamp Awarded

A heritage stamp is added to your binder with rarity, XP value, and visit date. Rarer sites = rarer stamps.

⭐

Earn XP

XP is automatically added to your passport. Level up to unlock special explorer titles.

🛠 Dev Note: GPS stamp detection uses the browser Geolocation API. In this prototype, stamps can also be manually collected from heritage site pages for judging purposes.
